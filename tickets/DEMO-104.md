# DEMO-104: Business insight access for executives

| Field     | Value                                   |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------|
| Type      | Access request                          |
| Requester | Alice Morgan (CEO)                      |
| Approver  | Chief Data Officer                      |
| Status    | Approved                                |

## Request

Executives, and AI agents acting for them, need the business-ready gold
tables to answer questions such as "What is driving the increase in Retail
Banking claims?"

## Acceptance criteria

- Members of the Executives group are subscribed to gold tables only.
- Executives get no bronze or silver (raw) data.
- Alice is a member of the Executives group.
- PII in gold tables stays masked.

## Out of scope

- Standing access to raw data.
