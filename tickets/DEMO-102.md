# DEMO-102: Move Brian Walsh from Insurance to Retail Banking

| Field     | Value                                   |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------|
| Type      | Change of role                          |
| Requester | HR                                      |
| Approver  | Head of Retail Banking                  |
| Status    | Approved                                |

## Request

Brian is moving from Insurance to Retail Banking. His access should follow
his new business unit.

## Acceptance criteria

- Brian's BusinessUnit attribute changes from Insurance to Retail Banking.
- Brian loses Insurance data and gains Retail Banking data.
- His region (APAC) is unchanged.
- No Immuta policy changes: access follows the attribute.

## Out of scope

- Any change for other users.
